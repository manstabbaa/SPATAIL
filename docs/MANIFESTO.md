# SPATAIL — Manifesto

We believe the best ideas deserve to exist beyond the screen.

For centuries, people have communicated through drawings, models, prototypes, and physical demonstrations. As our work has become increasingly digital, the tools we use to share ideas have remained surprisingly flat. Slides, PDFs, and static renders often struggle to communicate the scale, detail, and intent behind the things we create.

We are building a future where ideas can be experienced as naturally as they are imagined.

By combining spatial computing, generative AI, and thoughtful design, we enable products, spaces, and systems to become interactive presentations that exist in the real world. A concept can sit on a desk, a vehicle can occupy a room, and an architectural proposal can be explored at full scale — all while carrying the context, data, and story that give it meaning.

Our platform adapts to the people who use it. Whether you're an industrial designer, architect, engineer, marketer, or educator, your company's visual identity and design language become part of the experience. The presentation should feel like your brand, not ours.

Technology should disappear into the background. The focus should always remain on the idea itself.

We are building tools that make communication more human, more intuitive, and more spatial.

## The system

SPATAIL is organized around five pillars:

- **MATTER** — why it appears · what appears · how it appears
- **SPACE** — spatial mapping · placement principles · spatial takeover
- **IDENTITY** — brand graphic guide · render style guides · personalization
- **INTERACTION** — interaction rules · object behaviours · gestures
- **CREATION** — pipeline · craft · build

See [SYSTEM.md](SYSTEM.md) for how the codebase maps onto these pillars.
